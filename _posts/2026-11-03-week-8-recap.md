## Game of the Week: john's Scary Team Survives a Zero

**john's Scary Team** (John Matheson) beat **George Droyd** (Kyle Allen) **120.20 to 113.90** in their first-ever meeting. It did it with Lamar Jackson in the lineup for **0.0**, more than 22 points under his projection.

John's Scary Team was projected for 129.55 and came in well short. It didn't matter, because George Droyd had a lineup problem of its own.

## Bench Blunder of the Week: George Droyd

George Droyd only left **9.4 points** on its bench, less than half the league's typical week, but it was exactly enough to lose:
- Started **George Pickens** (6.8) over **Wan'Dale Robinson** (15.7)
- Started **Trevor Lawrence** (19.4) over **Kyler Murray** (19.9)

The Pickens call alone would have put George Droyd at 122.80, past john's Scary Team's 120.20. The best lineup was **123.30**.

George Droyd is now 1-7. Its all-play record is 25-46-1, which says it should have closer to three wins, the biggest gap between record and all-play in the league.

## Four Straight for john's Scary Team

The win is the fourth straight for john's Scary Team, which has scored at least 120 in every one of them, including the league-record 194.82 in week 5. At **5-3** it's tied with **Njigbas in Paris** (Erik Iverson) and **Mayeday** (Collin Gibbs), and its all-play record of 47-25 is second only to Njigbas in Paris.

They're all one game behind **Tiger's Wood** (Kevin Mangus), which finally ran out of soft landings. **GilberTeam** (Camilo Gelvez) beat it **120.38 to 67.22**, and Tiger's Wood's 67.22 was the lowest score of the week. It's still 6-2 and alone in first.

## Everyone Wants Jonah Coleman

Jonah Coleman was the week's biggest riser, up **36 points** in ESPN ownership to about 77% of leagues. **Don't call me Louis!** (Luis Barroso) has him, for the second time.

Back in week 3, Don't call me Louis! dropped Rome Odunze for Coleman, then let Coleman go less than a day later for Oronde Gadsden. Coleman spent the next several weeks on IR. Don't call me Louis! claimed him again on Wednesday, Oct. 28, then left him on the bench, where he scored 20.4. Last week it was Omar Cooper Jr. who scored 22.5 on that bench.

Don't call me Louis! lost to **Marino** (Andy Saliba) 130.30 to 93.00 and has lost three straight.

## Tyler Allgeier Gets Dropped, Then Gets Benched

Marino had Tyler Allgeier for three days. It claimed him on Sunday, Oct. 18, started him in week 6 for 5.8, and dropped him on Wednesday, Oct. 21, for Jordan Addison. (Addison didn't stick either: Marino cut him a week later for Dameon Pierce, then claimed him back on Nov. 4.)

**CmartDaDart** (Cayden Martin) picked Allgeier up on Friday, Oct. 23, started him in week 7 for 7.9, and then benched him this week behind Parker Washington (3.6). Allgeier scored **36.1**. It wouldn't have saved CmartDaDart, which lost **178.38 to 106.36** to Njigbas in Paris, but it's 36.1 points that neither of his last two teams got to use. CmartDaDart has lost three straight and is 4-4.
