## Welcome to Season Two

The Sunday Scrubs are back for year two, down to ten teams from twelve. Five teams start 1-0, including both of the league's new teams.

## Tiger's Wood Opens With 149

**Tiger's Wood** (Kevin Mangus) put up the week's top score, 149.46, and beat **CmartDaDart** (Cayden Martin) by 44.16, the week's biggest margin. **Caleb Williams** led the way with 37.26 on a 16.39 projection. Tiger's Wood also had the league's cleanest lineup, leaving only 3.1 points on the bench, and is now 3-0 all-time against CmartDaDart.

## 😬 Bench Blunder of the Week: GilberTeam

The defending champion's title defense started with a lineup to forget. **GilberTeam** (Camilo Gelvez) left **57.8 points on the bench** and lost to **Sleeper Cells** (Joseph Calaway) by 9.04:
- Started **Jadarian Price** (7.8) over **David Montgomery** (28.9)
- Started **A.J. Brown** (5.6) over **Deebo Samuel Sr.** (18.0)
- Started **Ja'Marr Chase** (3.2) over **Stefon Diggs** (15.5)

GilberTeam's best lineup would have scored **171.62**, enough to win easily and post the week's top score. Sleeper Cells didn't exactly earn it either: its 122.86 was below the league median, and it left Jalen Coker's 33.8 on the bench while starting Jordan Addison, who scored 0.0.

## The Rest of the Week

- **Don't call me Louis!** (Luis Barroso) beat **Mayeday** (Collin Gibbs) 143.16 to 124.42. Mayeday's 124.42 was the highest losing score of the week, and Jaylen Waddle didn't help (1.2 points).
- **john's Scary Team** (John Matheson), new to the league this year, beat **Marino** (Andy Saliba) 129.96 to 112.76, thanks to D'Andre Swift's 32.4.
- **Njigbas in Paris** (Erik Iverson), also new this year, beat last season's runner-up **George Droyd** (Kyle Allen) 129.86 to 122.70. Kenneth Walker III scored 34.1.

## Week 2 Preview

The pick of the week is a rematch of last season's championship game: **George Droyd vs. GilberTeam.** GilberTeam has won both of their meetings, the title game included. Also on the slate: Tiger's Wood vs. john's Scary Team, and Njigbas in Paris vs. Don't call me Louis!, both first-ever meetings.
