## Tiger's Wood Alone at the Top

The first-ever meeting of two 4-1 teams came down to 3.32 points. **Tiger's Wood** (Kevin Mangus) beat **Njigbas in Paris** (Erik Iverson) **116.04 to 112.72**, behind Jonathan Taylor (21.1), DK Metcalf (20.3) and Jordan Love (20.24).

CmartDaDart lost too, so Tiger's Wood is now alone in first at **5-1**, a game ahead of Njigbas in Paris and CmartDaDart. Its schedule has helped: it would be 33-21 if every team played every other team each week, while Njigbas in Paris would be 41-13, the best in the league.

At the other end, **George Droyd** (Kyle Allen) is finally on the board. It beat **Don't call me Louis!** (Luis Barroso) **115.34 to 107.98** for its first win of the season, led by Trevor Lawrence's 27.34, the best QB score of the week. George Droyd also won both of their meetings last season, so it's now 3-0 in the series.

And **GilberTeam** (Camilo Gelvez), the defending champion, snapped a three-game losing streak by beating **Mayeday** (Collin Gibbs) **93.92 to 85.50**. Only two teams scored less than GilberTeam this week, and one of them was Mayeday.

## Bench Blunder of the Week: CmartDaDart

**CmartDaDart** (Cayden Martin) lost to **john's Scary Team** (John Matheson) **128.28 to 115.34**, ending a four-game winning streak, and left 21.74 points on the bench:
- Started **Juwan Johnson** (0.0) over **Dallas Goedert** (12.1)
- Started **Davante Adams** (6.9) over **Khalil Shakir** (13.4)
- Started **Bryce Young** (19.84) over **C.J. Stroud** (22.98)

Its best lineup scored **137.08**, enough to win. The Juwan Johnson start stings most: Don't call me Louis! dropped him on 10/14 to add Germie Bernard, CmartDaDart claimed him the next day, and he scored 0.0. Bernard scored 25.7 for Don't call me Louis!, the second-best WR score of the week, and his ESPN rostered rate jumped 32 points, to 74.3%.

## The Waiver Wire Bites Back

**Mayeday** dropped **J.K. Dobbins** on 9/30 to add Tyreek Hill. Hill hasn't played a game since, and Dobbins has 36.2 points, now for Tiger's Wood, which claimed him two days later. That's the worst drop in the league right now.

**Njigbas in Paris** has one brewing too. It cut **Kyle Pitts Sr.** on 10/8 for Mike Gesicki, who has 7.4 points since and is back on waivers. Pitts has 38.2 since, and john's Scary Team, which claimed him on 10/14, got 23.0 from him this week on its bench.
