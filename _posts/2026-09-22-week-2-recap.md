## Standings Watch

**Njigbas in Paris** (Erik Iverson) and **Tiger's Wood** (Kevin Mangus) are the last two unbeaten teams. **George Droyd** (Kyle Allen) and **Marino** (Andy Saliba) are both 0-2.

## A League Record, With a Perfect Lineup

Njigbas in Paris scored **179.18**, the highest score in Sunday Scrubs history, and beat **Don't call me Louis!** (Luis Barroso) by 67.0, the biggest blowout of the season. Jaxon Smith-Njigba scored 42.5 on an 18.49 projection. The best part: Njigbas in Paris left **0.0 points on the bench.** Every lineup decision was right. That jumped them three spots to #1 in the power rankings.

## The Other End of the Record Book

**john's Scary Team** (John Matheson) went the other way: **65.10**, the lowest score in league history, in a 46.32-point loss to Tiger's Wood. It's also this week's Bench Blunder, with **40.88 points left on the bench**:
- Started **Luther Burden III** (6.2) over **Dalton Kincaid** (22.5)
- Started **Malik Nabers** (1.1) over **Xavier Worthy** (13.5)
- Started **Lamar Jackson** (14.8) over **Matthew Stafford** (26.98)

Even its best lineup (105.98) would have come up short of Tiger's Wood's 111.42. Tiger's Wood is 2-0, but that 111.42 was just below the league median (112.10), so it's the luckiest win of the week.

## The Title-Game Rematch

**GilberTeam** (Camilo Gelvez) beat George Droyd again, 115.56 to 90.76. That's GilberTeam's third straight win in the matchup, counting last season's championship game.

## The Rest of the Week

- **CmartDaDart** (Cayden Martin) scored 160.50 to beat **Sleeper Cells** (Joseph Calaway), and jumped seven spots in the power rankings to #3. Davante Adams scored 39.5 on a 15.5 projection, which more than covered Jaxson Dart's 0.8 at QB. Sleeper Cells' 133.82 was the highest losing score of the week.
- **Mayeday** (Collin Gibbs) beat Marino 112.02 to 97.68. Saquon Barkley managed 3.0 points for Marino on a 17.62 projection.

## Week 3 Preview

**Tiger's Wood vs. Sleeper Cells.** Tiger's Wood is unbeaten, but Sleeper Cells won both of their meetings last season. Also on the slate: CmartDaDart vs. George Droyd (George Droyd won their only meeting), and GilberTeam vs. Don't call me Louis!.
