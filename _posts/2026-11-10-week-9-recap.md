## Drop Regret of the Week: C.J. Stroud

On Thursday, Sept. 24, **Sleeper Cells** (Joseph Calaway) dropped **C.J. Stroud** for running back **Emanuel Wilson**. **CmartDaDart** (Cayden Martin) picked Stroud up on Sunday, Oct. 11, dropping Deshaun Watson to do it.

This week Stroud scored **35.36**, more than double his 16.95 projection, and CmartDaDart beat **GilberTeam** (Camilo Gelvez) **133.06 to 119.46**. He was the difference: with backup Bryce Young (20.34) in his place, CmartDaDart would have scored 118.04 and lost.

Wilson scored **0.0** this week. Sleeper Cells dropped him on Wednesday, Nov. 11, for Corey Kiner. The final tally on the swap: Wilson scored 65.3 points after Sleeper Cells claimed him, and Stroud has scored **130.56** since he was let go.

Sleeper Cells lost **173.44 to 105.86** to **Njigbas in Paris** (Erik Iverson), its third straight loss. It's 3-6.

One more CmartDaDart note: last week it benched Tyler Allgeier for 36.1. This week it started him, and he scored 6.6 while Parker Washington put up 14.1 on the bench. CmartDaDart won anyway, snapping a three-game skid, and is 5-4.

## Game of the Week: Five Straight for john's Scary Team

**john's Scary Team** (John Matheson) beat **Don't call me Louis!** (Luis Barroso) **119.78 to 111.38** in their first-ever meeting. It's the fifth straight win for john's Scary Team, the longest streak in the league this season.

It wasn't pretty. The 120-point streak ended at 119.78, and john's Scary Team came in 21.89 under its 141.67 projection. D'Andre Swift scored 3.1 against an 18.61 projection, and Luther Burden III scored 20.7 on the bench while Malik Nabers scored 9.3 in the lineup.

For Don't call me Louis!, the hot-pickup jinx finally broke: after benching Omar Cooper Jr. and Jonah Coleman the last two weeks, it started Coleman, who scored 18.8, and left just 2.8 points on its bench all week. It lost anyway. Jahmyr Gibbs scored 13.4, about half his 26.54 projection. That's four straight losses for Don't call me Louis!, which is 3-6.

john's Scary Team is 6-3, tied with Njigbas in Paris and **Mayeday** (Collin Gibbs), one game behind **Tiger's Wood** (Kevin Mangus). Tiger's Wood beat **Marino** (Andy Saliba) **113.88 to 97.48** behind Jordan Love's 36.18 and is alone in first at 7-2.

## Mayeday Keeps Winning Small

Mayeday beat **George Droyd** (Kyle Allen) **112.96 to 101.48**. The week's median was 113.42, so Mayeday won with a below-median score, by 0.46. It's the third time this season.

Mayeday is 6-3 with three straight wins, but its all-play record is 35-46. Only Tiger's Wood has outperformed its all-play record by more. Mayeday's opponents have scored 86.92 fewer points than the league average this season. George Droyd falls to 1-8. The series is now 1-1; George Droyd won last season's meeting by 0.26.

GilberTeam had the opposite week. Its 119.46 beat six of the ten teams, but it drew CmartDaDart and Stroud. GilberTeam is 4-5.

## Injuries: Jacobs and Nacua Ruled Out

Two rostered players were newly ruled out after week 9:

**Josh Jacobs** started for Marino this week and scored **0.5**, 16.11 under projection, while Saquon Barkley (8.2) sat on the bench. He's now listed as out. Marino is 4-5, and Harold Fannin Jr., Jakobi Meyers and T.J. Hockenson are all questionable.

**Puka Nacua** is out for john's Scary Team, a week after Lamar Jackson's zero. The streak will have to go through week 10 without him.
