## john's Scary Team Sets the League Record

**john's Scary Team** (John Matheson) beat **GilberTeam** (Camilo Gelvez) **194.82 to 127.84**, the highest score in league history. The old record was Njigbas in Paris' 179.18 from week 2.

The headliner was **Puka Nacua**, the first-round pick who had been one of the biggest busts of the draft: **37.7 points**. Isaiah Likely added 32.4, Malik Nabers 29.1, Matthew Stafford 27.32 and Brock Bowers 23.0. It also snapped a three-game losing streak, and john's Scary Team is now 2-3.

GilberTeam's 127.84 was the highest losing score of the week. The defending champion has lost three straight and is 1-4.

## Don't call me Louis! Scores Big, Regrets Bigger

**Don't call me Louis!** (Luis Barroso) beat **Sleeper Cells** (Joseph Calaway) **176.58 to 103.30**. That's the third-highest score in league history and the biggest blowout of the season (73.28). Jahmyr Gibbs scored 37.7 and Jacoby Brissett 25.28.

The waiver wire is another story. For the second week running, Don't call me Louis! has the league's worst drop: it cut **Rome Odunze** on 9/23 to add Jonah Coleman. Coleman hasn't scored a point since and is now on IR, while Odunze has 27.8. Odunze has since been claimed by GilberTeam, dropped six days later, and then claimed by Mayeday.

## Standings Watch

Three teams are 4-1:
- **Njigbas in Paris** (Erik Iverson) beat Mayeday 161.74 to 116.24 in their first-ever meeting.
- **CmartDaDart** (Cayden Martin) beat Marino 135.74 to 73.86, its fourth straight win.
- **Tiger's Wood** (Kevin Mangus) beat George Droyd 126.48 to 108.34. Its top scorer was **Jordan Love** (25.28), picked up on 9/24 for Marvin Harrison Jr. Across ESPN, Love's percent rostered jumped 29.6 points this week, to 78.2%.

At the bottom, **George Droyd** (Kyle Allen), last season's runner-up, is 0-5, and this one was self-inflicted. It started **Josh Downs (7.7)** while **Mike Evans scored 26.3 on the bench**. Its best lineup scored **126.94**, which would have beaten Tiger's Wood by 0.46. George Droyd still leads that series 2-1, after winning both meetings last season.
