## Bench Blunder of the Week: Njigbas in Paris

**Njigbas in Paris** (Erik Iverson) lost to **GilberTeam** (Camilo Gelvez) **121.70 to 101.02**, and its bench did most of the damage. It left **50.46 points** there:
- Started **Nico Collins** (2.6) over **Tee Higgins** (26.8)
- Started **Christian Watson** (10.7) over **Jameson Williams** (26.1)
- Started **Jared Goff** (18.02) over **Joe Burrow** (28.88)

Burrow's 28.88 was the best QB score of the week, and he watched from the bench. The best lineup was **151.48**, which would have beaten GilberTeam by nearly 30.

That's two straight losses for Njigbas in Paris, which has the league's most points and its best all-play record (45-18) and is somehow 4-3. It has now left 137.64 points on its bench this season.

## Tiger's Wood Wins Ugly, Again

**Tiger's Wood** (Kevin Mangus) beat **Don't call me Louis!** (Luis Barroso) **94.02 to 71.40**. That 94.02 was below the league median of 101.16 and beat only three teams this week, but it didn't need to beat more: Don't call me Louis! scored the second-lowest total of the season, more than 57 points under its projection.

That's three straight wins, and Tiger's Wood is **6-1**, two games clear of four teams at 4-3. Three of those six wins came with a below-median score. Its all-play record is 36-27, and its opponents have scored 124.26 fewer points than the league average this season, the friendliest schedule in the league.

The other side of the luck ledger: **George Droyd** (Kyle Allen) scored 101.30, just above the median, and still lost to **Marino** (Andy Saliba), which put up 122.22. George Droyd is 1-6.

## Everyone Wants Omar Cooper Jr.

Omar Cooper Jr. was the week's biggest riser, up nearly 30 points in ESPN ownership to 62% of leagues. Don't call me Louis! got him first, claiming him Wednesday, Oct. 21, for Terry McLaurin.

Then it left him on the bench, where he scored 22.5, the most of anyone on the roster. Instead it started Germie Bernard, last week's waiver hero, who scored 5.7, and Garrett Wilson (4.6).

Hunter Henry is the other name the league is chasing, up nearly 20 points to 66% owned. **Sleeper Cells** (Joseph Calaway) has him, and he scored 14.1 on its bench in a 128.30-92.48 loss to **john's Scary Team** (John Matheson).

## Mayeday Runs Away From CmartDaDart

Two contenders met, and only one showed up. **Mayeday** (Collin Gibbs) beat **CmartDaDart** (Cayden Martin) **127.34 to 78.22**, a 49.12-point margin. Drake Maye scored 27.44 for Mayeday, and Trey McBride added 27.1.

CmartDaDart was projected for just 95.10 and couldn't reach it. It started David Montgomery at RB for 0.0, and its bench had nothing better to offer. It's lost two straight and fallen from 4-2 to 4-3, level with Mayeday, which leads their all-time series 2-0.
