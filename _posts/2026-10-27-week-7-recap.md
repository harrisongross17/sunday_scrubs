## Bench Blunder of the Week: Njigbas in Paris

**Njigbas in Paris** (Erik Iverson) lost to **GilberTeam** (Camilo Gelvez) **121.70 to 101.02**, and the points to win it were sitting on the bench:
- Started **Nico Collins** (2.6) over **Tee Higgins** (26.8)
- Started **Christian Watson** (10.7) over **Jameson Williams** (26.1)
- Started **Jared Goff** (18.02) over **Joe Burrow** (28.88)

Those three swaps alone were worth **50.46 points**, enough to turn a 20.68-point loss into a comfortable win. KC Concepcion added another 23.2 on the bench for good measure.

It's a strange spot for the league's best team on paper. Njigbas in Paris has scored the most points in the league and has the best all-play record (45-18), but it has lost two straight and is 4-3.

## Tiger's Wood Keeps Finding Ways

**Tiger's Wood** (Kevin Mangus) beat **Don't call me Louis!** (Luis Barroso) **94.02 to 71.40**. Tiger's Wood's 94.02 outscored only three teams this week and was below the league median of 101.16, but Don't call me Louis! posted the week's lowest score, 57.31 under its 128.71 projection.

That's three straight wins, and Tiger's Wood is **6-1**, two games clear of four teams at 4-3. Three of those six wins came with a below-median score, and Tiger's Wood's opponents have scored 124.26 fewer points than the league average this season, the softest schedule in the league. Their all-time series is now 1-1.

At the other end of the luck scale, **George Droyd** (Kyle Allen) scored 101.30, just above the median, and still lost to **Marino** (Andy Saliba), which put up 122.22. George Droyd is 1-6, and Marino leads the series 2-0.

## Don't call me Louis! and the Waiver Wire

The drop-regret board has a familiar leader. Don't call me Louis! cut **Rome Odunze** in week 3 for Jonah Coleman. Odunze has **45.6 points** since, now for Mayeday, while Coleman has 8.9 and hasn't started a game for Don't call me Louis!. Its week 5 swap of Kendre Miller (23.8 since) for Dohnte Meyers (3.2) isn't going much better.

This week's pickup did work out, sort of. Don't call me Louis! added **Omar Cooper Jr.** on Wednesday, and ESPN's rostered rate for him jumped 29.7 points, to 62.27%. He scored **22.5**, on the bench, while Garrett Wilson (4.6) and Germie Bernard (5.7) started.

He wasn't the only riser who sat. **Hunter Henry**'s ESPN rostered rate climbed 19.65 points, to 65.63%, and he scored 14.1 on the bench for **Sleeper Cells** (Joseph Calaway) in a 128.30 to 92.48 loss to **john's Scary Team** (John Matheson).

## Mayeday Runs Away From CmartDaDart

**Mayeday** (Collin Gibbs) beat **CmartDaDart** (Cayden Martin) **127.34 to 78.22**, the week's biggest margin at 49.12. Drake Maye scored 27.44, with 27.1 from Trey McBride and 18.1 from Kyren Williams. CmartDaDart got 24.4 from CeeDee Lamb and not much else.

Both teams are now 4-3. CmartDaDart, which had won four straight before week 6, has now lost two in a row. Mayeday has won both of their meetings.
