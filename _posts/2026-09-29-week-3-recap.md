## Standings Watch

**Tiger's Wood** (Kevin Mangus) is the only unbeaten team left at 3-0. Four teams sit at 2-1 behind it. At the bottom, **George Droyd** (Kyle Allen), last season's runner-up, is 0-3.

## Tiger's Wood Survives

Tiger's Wood beat **Sleeper Cells** (Joseph Calaway) **127.58 to 123.06**, the closest game of the season (4.52). It was its first win over Sleeper Cells in three tries. Tiger's Wood also had the league's cleanest lineup for the second time in three weeks, leaving just 5.2 points on the bench.

There is some luck in the 3-0 start: Tiger's Wood's opponents have scored 71.92 points fewer than the league average so far. But it would be 18-9 if every team played every other team each week, so it's not all luck.

## A First Loss for Njigbas in Paris

**Marino** (Andy Saliba) had the week's top score, **157.58**, to hand **Njigbas in Paris** (Erik Iverson) its first loss. Bijan Robinson scored 35.3. The losing 134.22 was the highest losing score of the season so far.

## 😬 Bench Blunder of the Week: Mayeday (who won anyway)

**Mayeday** (Collin Gibbs) left **39.3 points on the bench**:
- Started **Justin Jefferson** (5.2) over **Michael Wilson** (25.9)
- Started **De'Von Achane** (1.7) over **Kyren Williams** (21.8)

It still won. Mayeday's 93.30 was the lowest winning score of the season, but **john's Scary Team** (John Matheson) managed only 85.14, its second straight loss.

## The Rest of the Week

- **Don't call me Louis!** (Luis Barroso) beat **GilberTeam** (Camilo Gelvez) 144.44 to 119.42. **Jahmyr Gibbs** scored 41.4, the best player week this week.
- **CmartDaDart** (Cayden Martin) beat George Droyd 125.24 to 111.28, its second straight win.

## Week 4 Preview

**Tiger's Wood vs. Mayeday.** The last unbeaten team against a team that keeps finding ways to win; Mayeday won their only meeting last season. Also on the slate: Don't call me Louis! vs. CmartDaDart (CmartDaDart is 2-0 in that matchup), and George Droyd vs. Sleeper Cells.
