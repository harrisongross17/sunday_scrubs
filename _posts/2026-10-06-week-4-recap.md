## Standings Watch

There are no unbeaten teams left. **Njigbas in Paris** (Erik Iverson), **CmartDaDart** (Cayden Martin), **Tiger's Wood** (Kevin Mangus) and **Mayeday** (Collin Gibbs) are all 3-1. **George Droyd** (Kyle Allen), last season's runner-up, is the league's only winless team at 0-4.

## Mayeday Ends the Streak

Mayeday beat Tiger's Wood **123.54 to 116.28**, its third straight win. Kyren Williams scored 36.7 on a 13.09 projection. Mayeday is now 2-0 all-time against Tiger's Wood.

It's also the league's luckiest team: 3-1, but only 15-21 if every team played every other team each week, with opponents scoring 45.78 points fewer than the league average.

## CmartDaDart's Big Week

CmartDaDart posted the week's top score, **151.86**, beating **Don't call me Louis!** (Luis Barroso) by 47.86. Tetairoa McMillan scored 45.2 on a 15.85 projection, the best player week of the week, and CeeDee Lamb added 41.3. That's three straight wins for CmartDaDart after a week 1 loss.

Don't call me Louis! has a drop regret that keeps getting worse: it dropped **Brian Robinson Jr.** in week 2 for Tyler Allgeier, and Robinson has outscored Allgeier 45.4 to 21.7 since.

## The Closest Game of the Season

Njigbas in Paris beat **john's Scary Team** (John Matheson) **148.22 to 145.78**, by just 2.44 points, the closest game of the season. That 145.78 is the highest losing score of the season, a tough way for john's Scary Team to lose a third straight game. Njigbas in Paris is back to #1 in the power rankings.

## Bench Blunder of the Week: Marino (which also won)

**Marino** (Andy Saliba) left **52.7 points on the bench**:
- Started **Rashee Rice** (0.0) over **Zay Flowers** (25.8)
- Started **DJ Moore** (2.7) over **Romeo Doubs** (23.8)

It still beat **GilberTeam** (Camilo Gelvez) 96.52 to 89.32, because GilberTeam left 52.6 on its own bench. The defending champion is 1-3 and leads the league in points left on the bench this season (142.1).

## The Rest of the Week

**Sleeper Cells** (Joseph Calaway) beat George Droyd 137.72 to 113.38, with a nearly perfect lineup: just 2.1 points left on the bench.

## Injury Watch

- **Tiger's Wood** has lost Caleb Williams, its week 1 star, for two straight games, and Travis Etienne Jr. is on IR.
- **Marino** still hasn't had Josh Jacobs for a single game.
- **Mayeday** picked up Zach Charbonnet, who has missed all four games so far.
- **Sleeper Cells** has had Isiah Pacheco on IR all season.
- **GilberTeam** has A.J. Brown on IR. Brown was its third-round pick and is one of the biggest busts of the draft so far, along with Puka Nacua (john's Scary Team's first-round pick, WR1 drafted, WR31 in points after missing two games).

## Week 5 Preview

**Tiger's Wood vs. George Droyd.** George Droyd is 0-4, but it won both of their meetings last season. Also worth watching: **Mayeday vs. Njigbas in Paris**, two 3-1 teams meeting for the first time.
